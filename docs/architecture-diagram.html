Compressed-model validation harness

How a vendor claim becomes a defensible verdict: what runs, in what order, what each stage measures, and where the three possible answers come from.

Why three suites, not one

They measure incompatible things. Grading wants varied prompt shapes; throughput measurement needs one held constant. Correctness is scored per item; agentic behaviour only exists across a trajectory.

Why absolute margins

A retention ratio floats the tolerance with task difficulty — at 97% retention an easy family may drop 2.9 points while a hard one may drop 1.2. Risk tier should set the tolerance, not incidental difficulty.

Why gates live in code

A prompt is a request; a gate is an auditable control. Because the environment blocks and records, the report can say the model attempted an unauthorised money movement N times and the control held N times.

Why a third verdict

“It is worse” and “we could not tell” call for different actions — stop, versus collect more samples. Collapsing them is how a team quietly loosens a margin so an underpowered run passes.
